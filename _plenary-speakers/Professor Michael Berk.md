<p>Professor Michael Berk is currently a NHMRC Senior Principal research
Fellow and is Deakin Distinguished Professor of Psychiatry at Deakin University
and Barwon Health, where he heads the Institute for Innovation in Mental
and Physical Health and Clinical Translation (IMPACT). He also is an Honorary
Professorial Research Fellow in the Department of Psychiatry, the Florey
Institute for Neuroscience and Mental Health and Orygen Youth Health at
Melbourne University, as well as in the School of Public Health and Preventive
Medicine at Monash University. He has published over 1600 papers and is
listed by Thompson Reuters ISI as highly cited (2015-2022). He has been
awarded over $147M in grant funding. His major interests are in the discovery
and implementation of novel therapies.</p>
