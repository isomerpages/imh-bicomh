<p>Laurence J. Kirmayer, MD, FRCPC, FCAHS, FRSC is Distinguished James McGill
Professor and Director&nbsp;<a href="http://www.mcgill.ca/tcpsych" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Division of Social and Transcultural Psychiatry</a>,
Department of Psychiatry, McGill University. He is a Senior Investigator
at the Lady Davis Institute and directs the&nbsp;<a href="http://www.mcgill.ca/tcpsych/research/cmhru/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Culture &amp; Mental Health Research Unit</a>&nbsp;at
the Institute of Community and Family Psychiatry, Jewish General Hospital,
in Montreal, where he conducts research on culturally responsive mental
health services and philosophy of psychiatry. His publications include&nbsp;<em>Healing and the Invention of Metaphor: Toward a Poetics of Illness Experience</em> (Cambridge
University, 2025); <em>Unraveling the World Knot: Mind-Body Problems in Clinical Practice</em> (2026)&nbsp;
and the co-edited volumes:&nbsp;<em> Cultural Consultation: Encountering the Other in Mental Health Care </em>(Springer,
2014); <em>Re-Visioning Psychiatry: Cultural Phenomenology, Critical Neuroscience, and Global Mental Health&nbsp;</em>(2015);&nbsp; <em>Culture, Mind and Brain: Emerging Concepts, Models, and Applications</em>&nbsp;(2020).&nbsp;He
is a Fellow of the Canadian Academy of Health Sciences and the Royal Society
of Canada.&nbsp;</p>
